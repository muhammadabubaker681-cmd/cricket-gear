Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about bat knocking-in, care, and warranties

❓ Do AERO bats require knocking-in before match play?

Yes! Although all our bats come factory pre-knocked, we recommend 10,000 gentle taps with a hardwood mallet on the edges and toe before facing new leather match balls.

❓ What is the difference between Grade 1+ and Grade 1 Willow?

Grade 1+ represents the top 1% of unblemished English Willow clefts with 9-12 clean straight grains. Grade 1 features 6-8 straight grains with equal power transmission.

❓ What is your return & warranty policy?

We offer a 30-day no-hassle return window and a full 1-Year Manufacturer Willow Warranty on handle and blade structural integrity.
